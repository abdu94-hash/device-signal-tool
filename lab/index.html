Case Lab: eight cases, ten steps each

Pick a case. Each case runs through all ten steps of the lifecycle. At each step you read the situation at the stated data cut, make one decision, and then see the solved answer with its working, the book's reasoning and the chapter to read. You cannot change an answer once it is revealed. Use Retry case to start a case again. Steps marked Worked in depth are the case's teaching focus. Brief steps stay qualitative and say what the dossier does and does not tell us.

    Your Signal: the ten-step workbench

    Take your own signal through the ten steps. Every field starts empty, and an empty field is never read as zero. Each step has a check button that validates the step and shows its result. Results are cleared as soon as you change an input. When the steps are complete, generate the signal record, print it or download it as an HTML file. Nothing is saved: copy the record into your document-controlled system.

    Replaces every field with the case's dossier data. Where the dossier holds no data, the example says so rather than inventing it.

      Generate the signal record

      The record checks every step again. It keeps the conclusion (confirmed, refuted or indeterminate) separate from the status, which is administrative. "Closed" is a status, not a finding.
