**Spreadsheet notes.** The formulas in T-6, T-7 and T-8 are live and recalculate when you open the file. Enter data only in the yellow cells. The grey chart-helper columns show #N/A for empty rows on purpose, so that the charts leave gaps instead of dropping to zero. The funnel limits in T-7 are valid for expected counts up to about 4,500.
